## 拿不准的题（18 处）

- **选择题强化训练（一） 第 37 题**：Mary asked her mother to ____ her at 8 next morning.  
  A. wake　B. awaken　C. waken　D. awake  
  预填 **A**。wake / waken 都能带宾语作「叫醒」讲，按最常用的 wake 选 A。
- **选择题强化训练（一） 第 49 题**：After studying Unit One, we all ____ to improve our study habits.  
  A. set down　B. set in　C. set up　D. set off  
  预填 **D**。四个选项都不贴切（最贴切的是 set out to do，选项里没有）。set off 是「出发、开始」，勉强选 D，请核对原书答案。
- **选择题强化训练（一） 第 61 题**：If you have worked hard ____, you won't have to sit up before the exams.  
  A. for the time being　B. from time to time　C. once in a while　D. at other times  
  预填 **B**。题意是「平时一直用功，考前就不用开夜车」，四个选项都不完全对应；from time to time（时常）最接近，选 B，请核对。
- **选择题强化训练（一） 第 65 题**：After having sat down on the chair, he took out a ____ of cigarettes.  
  A. parcel　B. package　C. packet　D. pack  
  预填 **C**。a packet of cigarettes（英式）与 a pack of cigarettes（美式）都对，按英式选 C。
- **选择题强化训练（一） 第 91 题**：I have spent all my pocket money on ____ books this year.  
  A. worth　B. worthwhile　C. worthy　D. worthless  
  预填 **D**。worthless（花在没价值的书上，带懊悔）和 worthwhile（花在值得读的书上）两个都说得通，预填 D，请按原书口径核对。
- **选择题强化训练（一） 第 104 题**：If you want to have some improvements in your study, there ____ to be some changes in your studying habits.  
  A. should　B. must　C. need　D. ought  
  预填 **D**。there ought to be / there need to be 都说得通，按最常见的 ought to 选 D。
- **选择题强化训练（一） 第 163 题**：The shop assistant ____ out the food on the counter.  
  A. displayed　B. spread　C. arranged　D. laid  
  预填 **D**。laid out 是「摆出、陈列」，选 D；spread out 也说得通。
- **选择题强化训练（一） 第 179 题**：The enemy camps ____ for ten miles.  
  A. spread　B. extended　C. stretched　D. expanded  
  预填 **C**。stretched 与 extended 都能接 for ten miles，选 C。
- **选择题强化训练（二） 第 6 题**：A new telescope that may reveal the new ____ has been set up.  
  A. universe　B. sky　C. orbit　D. space  
  预填 **C**。Claude 原先预填 A universe；用户拍来的原书手写答案是 C orbit（轨道），按手写答案改。
- **选择题强化训练（二） 第 14 题**：Although he has given us ____ information on that matter, we still can't come to a conclusion.  
  A. many　B. an　C. much　D. little  
  预填 **C**。Although 引出让步：给了很多信息仍下不了结论，选 C much。
- **选择题强化训练（二） 第 91 题**：Many authors use ____ names when they publish their books.  
  A. fake　B. favorite　C. flaw　D. false  
  预填 **D**。fake names 与 false names 都可以，按固定说法 false name 选 D。
- **选择题强化训练（二） 第 110 题**：The ____ typist had to retype the letter three times.  
  A. inefficient　B. ineffective　C. insensitive　D. incompetent  
  预填 **D**。incompetent（不称职的）与 inefficient（效率低的）都说得通，按「信重打三遍」偏能力问题选 D。
- **选择题强化训练（二） 第 147 题**：In his ____ to escape, the prisoner was frustrated by a watchful guard.  
  A. endeavors　B. experiences　C. attempts　D. experiments  
  预填 **C**。attempts 与 endeavors 都可，attempt to escape 最常见，选 C。
- **选择题强化训练（二） 第 190 题**：While George hadn't much experience in business, he ____ it with confidence.  
  A. went for　B. came about　C. went at　D. came under  
  预填 **C**。go at sth 是「卖力地干、全力以赴」，选 C。
